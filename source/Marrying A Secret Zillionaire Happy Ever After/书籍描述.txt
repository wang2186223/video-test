On her wedding day, Linsey was publicly abandoned by her fiancé Felix, who left her for his childhood sweetheart Joanna. Heartbroken, Linsey made a shocking decision on the spot: to marry Collin Riley, a mysterious wheelchair-bound stranger who had also been jilted at the altar.She thought she had married an outcast of a wealthy family, a bankrupt young master drowning in debt, unaware that her husband was the secret tycoon behind a global business empire. Faced with harassment from her ex, humiliation from his stepfamily, and difficulties at work, Linsey shed her weakness, fought back against her enemies, and rose step by step. Meanwhile, Collin silently guarded her, cleared all obstacles for her, and gave her all his love and tenderness.A flash marriage contract brought two lonely souls to redeem each other. From a contractual marriage to deep and unswerving love, she finally found a real home, and he regained the warmth of the world because of her.
