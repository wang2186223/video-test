One drunken night changes Dorothy’s life forever. After a one-night stand with her boss she finds herself agreeing to his marriage proposal. She never really tries to know the real reasons behind his proposal and makes up her own story.

Her supposition will make her reluctant to opening up to Everett, creating a series of mix-ups that will make their already frail marriage go downhill even faster than expected.

Dorothy’s story is unique and emotional as she finds herself falling for her boss, while believing that Everett only married her due to her resemblance with his real love. Will she finally open her eyes and see the truth?
