Joseph King 
Joseph King is an American author celebrated for his emotionally layered storytelling, relatable characters, and the quiet power of his prose. Known for blending contemporary realism with heartfelt emotional depth, King has built a devoted audience who find comfort, truth, and reflection in his writing. His novels often explore human resilience, family bonds, regret, redemption, and the complicated beauty of second chances.
Born in Sacramento, California, Joseph grew up in a modest household where books were treated like windows to the world. His mother, a literature teacher, encouraged nightly reading, while his father—a bus mechanic—taught him the value of persistence and humble hard work. Joseph often says his imagination “grew up in the public library,” where he spent countless afternoons filling notebooks with characters and worlds long before he knew he wanted to become an author.
King attended the University of Washington, majoring in English Literature and Creative Writing. During college, he won the campus short-fiction award twice, and professors frequently praised his instinctive ability to capture emotion with simplicity and grace. After graduation, he worked as a freelance editor, bookstore assistant, and part-time writing coach, slowly building a foundation for his literary career.
His breakout novel, Silent Branches, brought him national recognition. The story—about a fractured family attempting to rebuild after a painful loss—earned acclaim for its authenticity and lyrical beauty. Critics called King “a rare voice capable of turning everyday moments into profound emotional experiences.” The novel inspired book clubs across the U.S., and King became known as a writer who could make readers feel seen, understood, and deeply moved.
Following the success of Silent Branches, King published Half-Moon Harbor and Where the Night Still Breathes, both of which cemented his reputation as one of America’s most beloved contemporary storytellers. His works have since been translated into multiple languages, and he frequently speaks at literary festivals, universities, and creative-writing workshops.
Despite his success, Joseph remains grounded. He lives in Seattle, Washington, with his wife and their golden retriever, Winston. When he is not writing, he enjoys hiking coastal trails, collecting vintage typewriters, and teaching young writers who dream of telling stories that matter.
Joseph King believes that every ordinary life holds extraordinary meaning—and through his writing, he continues to remind the world of the quiet strength within the human heart.
Chapter 1 — Summary 
Story: Wolf romance ? Fated rejection ? Awakening wolf



Part 1 — A Night Meant for Love, Not Ruin
Avery had waited her whole life for Mating Day — when wolves turn 19 and find their destined mate.
Even without a wolf form, she believed she and Ryan — the Alpha heir — were meant to be.
They planned to choose each other before fate could interfere, mark each other, and build a life of love and protection.
She even wore secret lingerie.
She even believed tonight would be magic.
Instead, she opened his bedroom door to a nightmare.



Part 2 — Betrayal in Bed
Ryan was naked —
but not for her.
Her younger half-sister Zara straddled him, smirking, moaning theatrically, digging her teeth into his neck like she was performing for Avery.
Avery froze.
Her life shattered in one instant.
Ryan’s excuse?
“We just found out we’re fated mates.”
The word fated cut like a blade.
Destiny chose Zara — the girl who bullied her, humiliated her, laughed at her wolflessness — to take everything.



Part 3 — A Heartbreaking Truth
Zara coiled around Ryan like a snake.
With every breathy moan, she branded him as hers.
Ryan said what Avery had always feared:
“Fated mates make each other stronger.”
Translation:
you were never enough.
She trembled, holding back tears:
“So I was nothing to you?”
Her voice cracked.
Her heart split.
Ryan’s wolf surfaced, gold flickering in his eyes.
“Get lost, human.”
Not even Avery.
Not even a name.
Human.
Like she was nothing.



Part 4 — Running With a Broken Heart
She ran.
Tears slicing her face.
Her lungs burning.
Her world collapsing.
She sprinted deep into the forest, to her secret pond — the one peaceful place she’d ever had.
Where she once dreamed about her future.
She hugged her knees, shaking in agony, the earth soaking up her grief.
But Mating Moon night is dangerous — restless unmated wolves roam, heated, violent, unpredictable.
And Avery was alone.
Heartbroken.
Wolfless.
Unprotected.



Part 5 — Awakening Heat
Then it hit her — a scent.
Wild.
Powerful.
Addictive.
Heat exploded through her veins like fire.
Desire, instinct, raw and primal.
She gasped — body trembling, breath catching.
Heat?!
She never had a wolf.
She could never shift.
She was supposed to be wolfless.
So why was she burning from the inside?
This scent… this pull…
It felt like a fated mate bond awakening.
Impossible.
Terrifying.
Overpowering.



Part 6 — Danger in the Dark
Before she could understand, figures emerged from the woods — rogues.
Men with knives, sweat, hunger — and interest.
They smelled her heat.
They saw vulnerability.
They circled her like predators.
“A female wolf in heat.”
She trembled.
Threatened, cornered, overwhelmed.
“Stay away from me!”
They laughed.
Predators amused by prey.
One grabbed her.
She choked on fear.
No wolf.
No power.
No backup.
Just a girl abandoned twice in one night — by her mate and her world.



Part 7 — The Breaking Point
They reached for her clothes.
Her tears spilled.
Her voice shook.
Her body wanted to collapse.
Everything in her screamed for safety.
For help.
For the wolf she never had.
And suddenly — a roar ripped through the night:
“LEAVE HER ALONE!”
A voice like thunder and command.
The intoxicating scent intensified — drowning out the rogues, drowning out pain, flooding her senses with instinct, heat, and safety all at once.
Her knees nearly buckled.



Part 8 — The Alpha Presence
He emerged — unseen yet terrifying, powerful and furious.
The rogues froze — fear slicing through them.
This wasn’t just any wolf.
This was dominance.
Command.
Danger — and salvation.
Avery’s body reacted beyond control — heat darkening her vision, pulse racing, breath sharp.
Whoever he was, he was the source of the scent.
The heat.
The awakening inside her.
Was he—
Her fated mate?
Impossible.
Yet her soul howled toward his presence.



Part 9 — A Wolf Awakens
Her wolf — once silent, mocked, “dormant” — stirred.
A growl echoed inside her mind.
Alive.
Awakening.
Claiming.
The forest held its breath.
Everything Avery ever believed — that she was weak, wolfless, unwanted — shattered in that second.
Her wolf wasn’t gone.
Just waiting.
Waiting for him.



Part 10 — A New Destiny Begins
One night.
One betrayal.
One scent.
One voice.
Her world flipped.
Ryan wasn’t her destiny.
Zara wasn’t her end.
Her wolf wasn’t dead.
A stranger in the dark was the spark — and the storm.
The rogues shifted nervously.
Avery trembled between danger and desire, heartbreak and awakening.
And when the stranger spoke again, the ground itself seemed to bow:
“Touch her — and I’ll tear you apart.”
A mate bond stronger than betrayal.
A wolf rising from ashes.
A fate rewritten under the moon.
Avery’s story of pain had just become her story of power.
