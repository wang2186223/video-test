Werewolves didn't often seek education beyond primary school but Norah had already heard several now discussing the possibility applying to the pack for financial aid to allow them to attend college courses.

Jason was going to have to put serious thought into some sort of scholarship program and standardize a method for pack members to apply for educational funds. Norah knew he had been playing with the idea for awhile but if he wanted to keep up with his Luna he would have to implement it soon.

Human financial aid was unavailable to werewolves, vampires, fae and other supernatural species, another reason wolves generally didn't seek higher education.

Though packs were often quite well-off individual wolves rarely had more wealth than the average human a fact most humans didn't understand. However, that also meant there was plenty of opportunity for packs to support their members and there was no reason not to aid their educational goals.
