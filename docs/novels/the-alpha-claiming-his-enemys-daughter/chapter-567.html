He did.

The Silver Warriors were originally the warriors of this pack, thus of course, they would know every nook and cranny of this pack like the backs of their hands. If they decided to infiltrate this pack house, they would have the advantage to reach a certain place quicker than the Howling Wolf pack warriors and escape.

No one would believe how ferocious this bear could be if it was in battle mode.

"Yes, the reason why your concoction didn’t work was because there were traces of dark magic. The monsters were controlled by dark forces, that’s why it is hard for the wounds to heal." The same thing happened with Joel’s legs.

Some of the warriors were also injured during the monster attack, but because their injuries were not fatal like Joel and Lil Thing’s, the traces of dark magic gradually disappeared, while in Lil Thing and Joel’s case, it embedded deep into their system.
